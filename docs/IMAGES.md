## Client Custom Photography Replacement (Optional)
If the client wishes to substitute any of these photographs in the future with their own facility photos:
1. **Authenticity:** Shoot real yard, warehouse, and loading dock pictures. Do not use generic 3D renderings or stock photos of shiny consumer electronics.
2. **Lighting:** Bright natural daytime sunlight or heavy industrial halogen lighting.
3. **Format & Sizing:** Standard JPG or WebP (min 1200px width on long edge).
4. **Replacement:** Simply overwrite the matching filename in `assets/images/` without altering HTML code.
